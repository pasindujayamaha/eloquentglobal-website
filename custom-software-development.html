Custom Software FAQ

Questions businesses ask before starting a custom development project.

What is custom software development?

Custom software development is the design and engineering of software around a specific organisation, workflow, user group or business requirement rather than using a general-purpose packaged product.

When should a business choose custom software?

Custom software is usually worth considering when off-the-shelf tools create workflow limitations, require excessive manual work, cannot integrate with important systems, or cannot support a differentiated customer or operational experience.

How much does custom software development cost?

Cost depends on scope, user roles, workflows, integrations, platforms, data requirements, design complexity and technical architecture. We define the requirements before providing a project estimate.

How long does custom software take to build?

Timelines vary significantly by scope and complexity. A focused internal application can be much smaller than a multi-role platform with extensive integrations. We estimate delivery after discovery and requirements definition.

Can you integrate custom software with our existing systems?

Yes, where suitable APIs, databases or supported integration methods are available. Integration requirements are reviewed during discovery and technical planning.

Can you improve or replace an existing system?

Yes. Depending on the current product, we can review an existing application, modernise parts of it, add functionality, integrate new services or plan a replacement system.

Do you work with US and European businesses?

Yes. Eloquent Global is based in Sri Lanka and works through a remote delivery model designed for international collaboration, including businesses in the USA, UK and wider Europe.
