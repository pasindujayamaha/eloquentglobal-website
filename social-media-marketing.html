Social Media Marketing FAQ

Questions businesses ask before choosing a social media marketing agency.

What does a social media marketing agency do?

A social media marketing agency helps businesses plan and execute social strategy, content, campaigns and channel activity around defined audiences and marketing objectives.

Which social media platforms should my business use?

That depends on your audience, market, offer, content requirements and business objectives. We prefer selecting channels based on fit rather than assuming every business needs every platform.

Can you create a social media content strategy?

Yes. Social strategy can cover audience priorities, channel roles, content themes, messaging and campaign direction according to the engagement.

Can social media be part of a wider digital marketing engagement?

Yes. Social media can be combined with broader digital strategy, paid campaigns, search and creative work through our Digital Marketing service.

Do you guarantee followers, engagement or sales?

No. Social outcomes depend on the brand, audience, offer, content, competition, budget, platform conditions and other factors. We focus on structured strategy, execution and iteration.

How much do social media marketing services cost?

Cost depends on channels, content volume, creative requirements, campaign scope and engagement duration. We review the requirements before defining commercial terms.
