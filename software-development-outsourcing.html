Software Development Outsourcing Services

Add delivery capacity where your internal team needs it.

Software Outsourcing FAQ

Questions companies ask before outsourcing development.

What is software development outsourcing?

Software development outsourcing means engaging an external company to deliver some or all of the engineering work for a software product, system or roadmap.

Can you work alongside an existing development team?

Yes. An engagement can be structured around defined workstreams or additional engineering capacity alongside an internal product or technology team.

Can we outsource an entire software product?

Yes, where the product requirements and responsibilities are suitable for an end-to-end engagement. Scope, design, engineering, QA and delivery responsibilities are defined during planning.

Is Eloquent Global an offshore software development company?

Eloquent Global is based in Sri Lanka and provides remote software development services to international clients. For companies in the USA, UK and Europe, this can function as an offshore or international outsourcing relationship.

Who owns the source code and deliverables?

Source-code access, deliverables and ownership terms are defined in the project agreement and handover arrangements for each engagement.

How much does software development outsourcing cost?

Cost depends on scope, team requirements, engagement model, technology, integrations, product complexity and duration. We review the requirement before providing commercial terms.
