Good Automation Candidates

Look for repeated work, clear inputs and valuable time savings.

High-volume repetitive tasks

Processes repeated daily or weekly with similar inputs and predictable outcomes.

Information-heavy workflows

Tasks involving reading, categorising, summarising or transferring information.

Disconnected business systems

Work that requires people to manually move data between applications.

Approval-based processes

Workflows where automation can prepare or route work while a human retains final control.

AI Development is for companies building AI-powered software, customer-facing products, AI agents or new AI-enabled product capabilities.

AI Automation FAQ

Questions businesses ask before automating a workflow.

What are AI automation services?

AI automation services use AI together with workflow logic, integrations and business rules to reduce manual work in repeatable business processes.

What business processes can be automated with AI?

Potential candidates include information classification, document handling, enquiry routing, drafting, summarisation, data transfer, follow-up and other repeatable workflows. Suitability depends on the process and available systems.

Does every automation need AI?

No. Some steps are better handled with deterministic rules or traditional software automation. We use AI where language understanding, flexible classification or generation is useful.

Can AI automation connect to our existing software?

Where suitable APIs or supported integration methods are available, automation can connect with existing tools and systems. Integration feasibility is reviewed during discovery.

Can a human approve actions before they happen?

Yes. Human review and approval can remain part of the workflow where the process requires oversight or accountability.

How much does AI automation cost?

Cost depends on workflow complexity, systems involved, integrations, data, user roles, AI usage and the controls required. We scope the process before providing an estimate.
