Measurement

Performance metrics should be interpreted in the context of the business goal.

Reach and impressions

Useful for understanding distribution, but not sufficient on their own to establish business performance.

Clicks and click-through rate

Indicate interaction with ads while still requiring downstream context.

Conversions and conversion rate

Can indicate completed tracked actions when measurement is correctly configured and the action reflects the campaign objective.

Cost metrics

Cost per click or tracked acquisition can support decisions when interpreted alongside lead quality, margins and business economics.

PPC FAQ

Questions businesses ask before choosing a PPC agency.

What does a PPC agency do?

A PPC agency helps plan, structure, manage and optimise paid advertising campaigns according to agreed objectives, audiences, channels and available performance data.

What is pay per click management?

Pay per click management covers the ongoing work involved in paid campaigns, which can include account structure, targeting, search terms or audiences, ad messaging, budgets, measurement and optimisation.

Do you provide Google Ads management?

Yes. Google Ads management can be included where the platform and campaign types fit the agreed marketing requirement.

Do you provide paid social advertising?

Yes. Paid social campaigns can be planned and managed for applicable social platforms according to audience, creative, account access and campaign scope.

Is PPC the same as performance marketing?

PPC is one form of paid digital advertising. Performance marketing is a broader approach that emphasises measurable actions and campaign performance across applicable paid channels.

Do you guarantee a specific ROAS or number of leads?

No. Paid campaign results depend on the offer, competition, audience, budget, creative, landing experience, tracking, market conditions and other factors. We do not guarantee a specific return, lead volume or acquisition cost.

How much do PPC services cost?

Management cost and media spend depend on campaign scope, channels, markets, creative requirements and account complexity. Advertising spend is also distinct from agency service fees unless commercial terms explicitly state otherwise.
