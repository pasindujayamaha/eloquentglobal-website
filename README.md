# Eloquent Global

**We build digital products and help businesses grow them.**

This repository contains the official website for [Eloquent Global](https://eloquentglobal.com/), a technology and digital growth company established in 2019 and based in Moratuwa, Sri Lanka.

## What we do

Eloquent Global works with businesses across software engineering, artificial intelligence, digital marketing, and long-term engineering partnerships.

- **Software Development** — web platforms, business systems, applications, and custom digital products.
- **AI Solutions** — practical AI and automation designed around real business workflows.
- **Digital Marketing** — digital strategy and execution focused on measurable business outcomes.
- **Engineering Partnership** — ongoing technical collaboration for companies that need reliable product and engineering capacity.
